DICOM to NIfTI

Convert MRI DICOM to NIfTI (.nii.gz) with dcm2niix. Conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

1. Choose your DICOM

Drop a folder or a .zip here

or

No DICOM handy? Download the UK Biobank example brain T1 (39 MB .zip, from UK Biobank) and drop the .zip above without unpacking it.

A whole study folder is fine; localizers and other series are listed separately so you can pick. Very large studies (over ~1.5 GB) may exhaust browser memory. If that happens, select just the series folder you need.
